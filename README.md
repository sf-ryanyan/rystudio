## 👋 About me · 关于我

I'm **Ryan Yan**, a certified professional photographer based in the San Francisco Bay Area.

I started with landscapes. Waiting two hours on a ridge for one band of light taught me
most of what I know about photography: patience, timing, and reading the light before
it arrives. I bring that same patience to every portrait, wedding and commercial shoot.

> 我是 **Ryan Yan**，一名认证专业摄影师，常驻旧金山湾区。
> 我从拍风光起步。为一束光在山脊上等两个小时，教会了我摄影里最重要的几件事：
> 耐心、时机，以及在光到来之前就读懂它。这份耐心，我同样用在每一次人像、婚纱和商务拍摄上。

## 🌅 How I work · 我的拍摄方式

- **Light first · 光线优先** — every shoot is planned around golden hour or blue hour,
  not around a fixed clock. 每次拍摄都围绕黄金时刻或蓝调时刻来安排，而不是固定的钟点。
- **One clear quote · 明确报价** — every job is quoted on its own: a real number, not a range.
  每个项目单独报价，给明确数字，不给区间。
- **Bilingual · 中英双语** — we can talk it through in English or Chinese.
  中文、英文都可以沟通。
- **Fast reply · 快速回复** — inquiries are answered within 24 hours.
  询价 24 小时内回复。
