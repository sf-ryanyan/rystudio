FAQ

Before you book预约之前

How much does a shoot cost?拍摄多少钱？

Every shoot is quoted individually, because hours, location and the number of final images all change the price. Send your date and what the photos are for, and a real number comes back — usually the same day.

每次拍摄单独报价，因为时长、地点和成片数量都会影响价格。把日期和用途发给我，我会回一个明确的数字，通常当天就回。

Where will we shoot?在哪里拍？

I pick it. The choice comes from what the photos are for, the season, and where the light will be that day. You do not have to research anything — if you already have a place in mind, even better.

我来选。看照片的用途、看季节、看那天的光在哪边。你完全不用做功课；当然，你自己有想去的地方更好。

How long until I get the photos?多久能拿到照片？

A preview selection comes first so you can choose, then the full retouched set. The exact timeline is written into your quote, so you know before you book.

先给你一组预览照片选片，之后交付全部精修成片。具体天数会写进报价里，定之前你就知道。

What if it rains?下雨怎么办？

Outdoor shoots move to another date at no charge. And bad weather is not always bad news — overcast light is softer than sun, and fog is worth going out for.

外景可以免费改期。而且天气差不一定是坏事 —— 阴天的光比大太阳柔和，起雾的日子更是值得专门跑一趟。

Can we talk in Chinese?可以用中文沟通吗？

Yes. English or Mandarin, whichever is easier for you — including on the day of the shoot.

可以。中文、英文都行，拍摄当天也一样。
